## 적용 아이디어

### 원리 #3 국소적 품질 (관계/조건 분리)
Sub-principle 1: replace a uniform structure with one that differs from region to region.
- **Idea 1:** Use a non-uniform guard. Make the cells reachable by a hand small enough that a finger cannot enter. Make the bars thin and streamlined, so the open area stays high. A thin bar blocks a finger but blocks little air.
- **Idea 2:** Put the finest mesh only on the outer ring where hands approach. Leave the zone directly above the rotor disk more open. Check this zone against the finger-reach test first.
- Analogy only (case: `mechanical`, a hardened gear surface with a tough core). Each region gets the property it needs.

### 원리 #9 사전 반대 조치 (시간 분리)
Sub-principle 2: load the part in advance with the opposite stress.
- **Idea 1:** Add shutter louvers inside the guard. A spring pre-loads them to the closed position. At rest and at low rotor speed they stay closed, so a finger cannot pass. In flight, the airflow overcomes the spring and opens them. The spring is the pre-applied opposite action.
- **Idea 2:** Choose the spring force so the louvers open fully at hover thrust. They then add little drag in flight.
- Analogy only (case: `mechanical`, pre-pressurized tube). A force applied in advance changes the behavior in service.

### 원리 #17 다른 차원으로 (관계/조건 분리)
Sub-principle 1: move from a single line to layers. Sub-principle 2: tilt the object.
- **Idea 1:** Stack two or three thin grille layers, with each layer offset from the one below. Each layer has large gaps. A straight finger path cannot go through all layers. Air can follow a short zigzag path.
- **Idea 2:** Tilt the slats. Air flows through at an angle. A finger cannot pass in a straight line.
- **Idea 3:** Make the guard deep. Narrow, tall cells, like a honeycomb, keep a finger far from the blade. The cells still pass air along their axis.
- Analogy only (case: `electronics`, vertical cell stacks). Use the third dimension instead of a larger area.

### 원리 #10 사전 조치 (시간 분리)
Sub-principle 1: do the needed change before it is required.
- **Idea 1:** Close the guard before hands can come near. Close the shutters during descent, before the handoff. Open them again only after the drone is away from people.
- **Idea 2:** Do not start the rotors until the guard is closed. Add a mechanical interlock for this.
- **Idea 3:** Use a proximity or hand detector to trigger early closing. This needs a separate safety check.
- Analogy only (case: `medical`, defibrillators placed in advance). The protective state is ready before the risk appears.

### 원리 #19 주기적 조치 (관계/조건 분리)
Sub-principle 1: replace a continuous action with a periodic pulsed action.
- **Idea 1:** Add a slowly rotating outer ring with slots. Each slot opens for a short time only. A finger cannot enter during that window. Air still passes through the open slots, so the average open area stays large.
- **Idea 2:** Add a risk analysis before use. A moving ring is a new hazard, such as a pinch point. This idea has the lowest confidence of the five.
- Analogy only (case: `mechanical`, drilling with withdrawals). A pulsed action replaces a continuous one.
